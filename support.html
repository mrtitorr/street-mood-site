Street Mood Support

Street Mood is a game about sharing a feeling with the neighbors. It plays offline. There are no accounts, no ads, and no chat.

The game does not collect personal information. Stars, the Pip look, and settings stay on the device.

To open Settings, Restore Purchases, or Privacy, tap Settings and then tap the number 7. That grown-up check is the parent gate. There is no password.

The free download includes Little Kids places 1–5 and street places 1–3. Full Game is one optional purchase, product id com.streetmood.kids.full, priced at $0.99. It unlocks the rest of the places. Apple handles the payment. Erase stars does not remove Full Game. Restore Purchases is in Settings, behind the grown-up check.

For help, email support@streetmood.app.
