Street Mood Privacy

Street Mood does not collect personal information. The game plays offline. It does not need an account, a network, or a sign-in.

There are no ads. There is no tracking. There is no chat. This version of the app does not connect to other players.

Stars, the Pip look, and settings stay on the device.

The download includes Little Kids places 1–5 and street places 1–3. Full Game is one optional purchase, product id com.streetmood.kids.full, priced at $0.99. Apple handles the payment. The game does not see a card or an Apple ID.

Erase stars clears progress on the device. It does not remove Full Game.

The game does not open other web pages from inside play. Settings, Restore Purchases, and the in-app privacy note sit behind a grown-up check. A grown-up taps the number 7.

Questions about privacy: support@streetmood.app
